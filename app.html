Operational Trade-Off & Decision Analysis

In the baseline scenario, Logistics agents carry +14.5% higher individual resolving volume while managing courier delays that require multiple touchpoints. Billing handles short-duration queries (1.23h median) but experiences high transfer overhead from misrouted delivery tickets.

Why Intake Queue and Resolving Workload Tell Different Stories:

Intake volume reflects where the chatbot placed the ticket based on initial keywords. Resolving workload reflects where the work was actually performed. At intake, Billing appears +520 tickets larger. At resolution, Logistics handles +776 more tickets. Hiring into Billing without fixing intake routing funds capacity for tickets that Billing agents simply hand off to Logistics.
